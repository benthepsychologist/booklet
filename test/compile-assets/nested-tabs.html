A walk in the park

A made-up page: tabs inside tabs, and two activities that each hold folds.

Plan #

Choose a route and pack the bag before you set out.

Choose a route #

Three loops start at the gate. For wet weather see the wet path.

Short loop #

A short loop takes twenty minutes and stays on the flat. See also Hilly loop.

Long loop #

The long loop circles the pond and takes an hour.

Wet ground

After rain the far side of the pond is soft.

Hilly loop #

The hilly loop climbs to the lookout and back.

Take poles

Walking poles help on the steep stretch.

What to bring #

Pack light.

A thermos

Hot tea keeps well for an hour.

A hat

A brim keeps the sun off.

A hint

Choose what is easy to eat while walking.

A solution

There is no wrong snack.

During #

Keep to the path and watch the weather. The hilly loop is back under Plan.

On the path #

Say hello, and give way to bikes.

Dry path #

The dry path is firm all the way round.

Wet path #

The wet path has boards over the muddy stretch.
