Gathering #

Pick leaves on a dry afternoon, when the dew has gone.1 Choose ones with no holes, and keep each flat between two sheets of paper on the way home.

Tip

Carry a book

An old paperback makes a good press for the walk back.

  • Maple turns red in autumn.
  • Fern keeps its green.
  • Ginkgo goes a clear yellow.

Pressing #

Lay each leaf between blotting sheets, then stack heavy books on top. A press needs about m≥5 kilograms.

Leaf Days Weight (kg)
Maple 7 5
Fern 10 6
Ginkgo 5 4
More about blotting paper

Change the sheets after two days, so the leaves dry and do not mould.

Drying #

Patience is the whole method.

Leave the stack alone. A site template would read {{ .Title }} and {% raw %} as code, so the compiler writes them as text.

Keeping #

Glue the dry leaves into a notebook. For the next step, go back to Gathering or the top, or try A kite workshop. A museum's own guide goes further.
