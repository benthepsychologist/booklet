A made-up booklet for the compiler's tests, in two languages: a soft egg and a hard one.

Eggs #

  1. 1
    A soft egg

    Lower the egg into boiling water for six minutes, then cool it under the tap.

  2. 2
    A hard egg

    Lower the egg into boiling water for ten minutes, then cool it under the tap. Compare it with A soft egg.
