How tides work

Why the sea rises twice a day

Most harbours on this coast see two high tides and two low tides each lunar day.1 The range is the difference between them: R=hhigh−hlow.

Differs from your guide

Your guide says the tide turns every six hours

The guide rounds it off. High water comes a little later each day.1

Spring and neap #

Twice a month the sun and the moon pull together, and the range is at its widest.
