# If you need help now

**The crisis resources and the scope note.** Not an ordinary activity — a
*pinned* one: the renderer keeps it one tap away from every page.

⚠️ **These numbers are clinical content and jurisdiction-specific.** Ruled
approved as written (Ben, 2026-09-08): 9-8-8, Kids Help Phone, ConnexOntario,
Info-Social 811, 1-866-APPELLE, 911. Do not edit them as copy.

⚠️ **If you are adapting this for somewhere other than Ontario and Québec,
replace the numbers.** They are the whole point of the module and they are
jurisdiction-specific; a panel offering a reader the wrong country's crisis
line is worse than no panel.

## Adding it to a booklet

Open this file on its own, or copy everything below the front matter into
your own booklet, above the long rule. It will not appear as a card on the
home page: it is a pinned panel, reachable from wherever you are.

> [!module|mensio-help-now] If you need help now

Help now.

> [!activity|help pinned] If you need help now

This page is not watching, and it cannot help in a crisis. If you are in danger, or thinking about ending your life, contact one of these instead. They are free, confidential, and answer at any hour.

> [!warning] If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

## Anywhere in Canada

- **9-8-8 — Suicide Crisis Helpline** — Call or text 988. Around the clock, in English and French.
- **Kids Help Phone** — 1-800-668-6868, or text CONNECT to 686868. For young people, around the clock.

## Ontario

- **ConnexOntario** — 1-866-531-2600. Free information and referral for mental health, addiction, and problem gambling services, around the clock.

## Québec

- **Info-Social — 8-1-1** — Dial 811 and choose option 2 for psychosocial support, around the clock.
- **1-866-APPELLE** — 1-866-277-3553. Québec's suicide prevention line, around the clock.

## What this page is, and what it is not

This is psychoeducation — general educational material about how people tend to work. It is not assessment, diagnosis, therapy, or medical advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

It knows nothing about you. Nothing you write is read by anyone, scored, or interpreted. The counts it shows you are counts of what you selected, not measurements of how you are doing, and nothing here is a diagnosis.

If something here does not fit your situation, it does not fit. Trust that over the page.
